Swipe Night

A cyber-noir world in technological decay. A broke and desperate tech developer faking a lifestyle using women on dating apps to get Wi-Fi in order to complete the perfect artificial intelligence. He's trapped in a technocratic surveillance state — can he finish before he's caught, or will he become the target of what he seeks to destroy?

The World

Swipe Night is set in a cyber-noir world of technological decay — a neon-soaked technocratic surveillance state where tropical deco architecture glows under constant drone watch. Every connection is transactional, every signal is tracked, and survival depends on staying one step ahead of the system.

Cyber-Noir Visuals

Classic 1940s moody shadows and high-contrast framing collide with neon tropical deco and a suffocating dystopian landscape. Palm-lined streets glow under surveillance drones while decaying storefronts and holographic ads define a world that feels both timeless and uncomfortably current.

High-Concept Thriller

A broke tech developer fakes a lifestyle on dating apps to steal Wi-Fi and finish the perfect AI — all while trapped in a technocratic surveillance state. Algorithmic isolation, data vulnerability, and a race against being caught drive a lean, tense narrative built for maximum impact.

Lean Production

Engineered from page to screen for maximum visual impact, using strategic real-world locations and stylized world-building. Designed to look expensive while remaining efficient and achievable — high concept, controlled scope, and a signature neon-tropical aesthetic.

Brand Integration & Partnerships

We are offering select local businesses and brands high-visibility placement within the visual landscape of Swipe Night. From neon-soaked city streets to intimate set pieces, your brand can live inside the world of the film in a way that feels organic, cinematic, and unforgettable.

Real Estate & Commercial

In-universe holographic real estate banners, digital billboards, and property placement featured prominently in key outdoor city sequences. Ideal for developers, brokerages, and commercial spaces looking for elevated exposure in a cyber-noir environment.

Apparel & Wardrobe

Tailored menswear, custom street style, and high-fashion wardrobe integrations for lead characters throughout the film. Perfect for designers, boutiques, and lifestyle brands that want their pieces worn on screen in high-impact moments.

Food, Tea & Beverages

Custom packaging and branded product placement inside pivotal set locations, including the Zion 7 cafe and local storefronts. A natural fit for cafes, tea brands, craft beverages, and local food concepts.

Additional partnership categories are available, including tech products, vehicles, lifestyle accessories, and more. Packages can be tailored to your budget and goals — from subtle product placement to featured brand moments.
